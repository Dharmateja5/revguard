RevGuard

Autonomous revenue leakage investigation across contracts, usage, billing, CRM, approvals, and optional commercial-call evidence—with deterministic financial controls, policy enforcement, HITL, and auditable recovery.

Architecture thesis: Agents investigate. Deterministic systems verify, calculate, authorize, and execute.
