Portfolio Project

Supplier Commitment Exception Resolution

Autonomous investigation and recovery planning for supplier commitment failures across purchase orders, inventory, production demand, logistics, alternate sourcing, supplier communications, and approvals—with typed Jev tool calling, deterministic policy controls, HITL, sandbox execution, verification, and auditable state.

Architecture thesis: Agents investigate and adapt. Jev structures decisions and tool calls. Deterministic systems validate, authorize, execute, and verify.
