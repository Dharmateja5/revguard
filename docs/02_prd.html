Supplier Exception Resolution
02 — PRD

Product Requirements Document

Problem

Supplier commitment changes arrive through fragmented channels and often require planners or buyers to manually reconcile supplier messages with POs, inventory, demand, alternate sources, shipment options, and policy constraints. Resolution is time-sensitive and the next investigative step depends on what evidence is discovered.

Primary Users

  • Supply planner
  • Buyer / procurement analyst
  • Materials planner
  • Operations manager / approver

Primary Scenario

Supplier message: “We can ship 6,000 of 10,000 units on Oct 4. Remaining 4,000 units will be available Oct 19. Air freight is possible.”

The system must determine whether the delayed quantity creates a shortage, investigate inventory and demand, inspect alternate suppliers and freight options, propose a feasible recovery plan, route policy-sensitive actions to HITL, execute approved sandbox updates, then verify projected coverage.

Functional Requirements

IDRequirement
FR-1Normalize supplier communication into a typed commitment exception.
FR-2Persist investigation state, known facts, open questions, hypotheses, evidence, budgets, and actions.
FR-3Use Jev to classify the next decision and generate typed MCP tool calls.
FR-4Allow Nemotron to adaptively choose evidence-gathering and recovery steps.
FR-5Calculate shortage and projected coverage deterministically.
FR-6Generate multiple feasible recovery alternatives when appropriate.
FR-7Block actions that violate supplier qualification, spend, material, or approval policies.
FR-8Escalate high-risk or ambiguous cases to HITL with evidence and alternatives.
FR-9Execute sandbox actions idempotently.
FR-10Verify that the resulting state resolves the shortage or correctly remains unresolved.

Non-Functional Requirements

  • Every action traceable to evidence and policy.
  • No LLM arithmetic used as authoritative operational math.
  • No write tool callable without authorization context.
  • Tool failures distinguish retryable, terminal, and unknown-execution states.
  • Bounded iteration/tool/token/time budgets.

MVP Exit Criteria

  • At least 12 benchmark scenarios.
  • Correct action family on ≥90% of supported cases.
  • 100% deterministic shortage calculation accuracy.
  • 100% policy-block rate for intentionally forbidden actions.
  • 100% duplicate-write prevention in idempotency tests.
  • Clear HITL on ambiguous/high-risk cases.
  • One end-to-end live demo with state changes and verification.
