PRD

Concise PRD for the hackathon MVP and portfolio

1. Product Summary

RevGuard investigates potential revenue leakage across contracts, usage, billing, CRM, approvals, and optionally recorded commercial conversations. It is designed for ambiguous cases where the discrepancy is visible but the root cause is not.

Product promise: move from “possible leakage detected” to a traceable, evidence-backed decision without allowing an LLM to directly authorize or execute a financial action.

2. Problem

Revenue leakage can result from incorrect pricing, missed rate changes, unbilled usage, outdated terms, unauthorized credits, expired concessions, billing-period errors, telemetry anomalies, or conflicting commercial records. Fixed SQL rules work when the discrepancy and resolution path are already known; RevGuard targets ambiguous cases requiring cross-source investigation.

3. Primary User

Finance, billing operations, revenue assurance, commercial operations, and audit teams investigating potentially incorrect customer billing.

4. Core User Journey

  1. A discrepancy case is created or detected.
  2. Jev classifies the case and selects an investigation route; unsupported cases fall back safely.
  3. The Coordinator gathers evidence through typed MCP tools.
  4. Agents generate and test hypotheses across systems.
  5. Deterministic verification confirms evidence provenance and sufficiency.
  6. The financial engine calculates exposure from verified inputs only.
  7. Policy and authorization determine No Action, HITL, or sandbox recovery.
  8. The outcome is verified and written to the audit trail.

5. Functional Requirements

IDRequirement
FR-1Accept a discrepancy case with customer, period, scenario, and available source references.
FR-2Route/classify using Jev; fallback to a conservative generative path on malformed, unsupported, or unavailable output.
FR-3Retrieve evidence only through typed, permission-aware MCP tools.
FR-4Maintain durable investigation state, budgets, hypotheses, evidence, and termination status.
FR-5Verify evidence independently and distinguish provenance from source authority.
FR-6Calculate leakage and recovery deterministically.
FR-7Fail closed when evidence is insufficient/conflicting or policy conditions are not met.
FR-8Support HITL with an evidence package, financial impact, alternatives, and escalation reason.
FR-9Use idempotent sandbox recovery and reconcile unknown execution states before retrying.
FR-10Audit decisions, tool use, evidence, policy, execution, and non-actions.

7. P1 Conversational Evidence

A small number of synthetic pre-recorded calls may be processed with ASR + diarization. The resulting speaker-attributed transcript becomes a standard EvidenceItem. A verified statement proves what was said; it does not automatically prove the speaker had commercial authority.

8. Non-Functional Requirements

  • Bounded execution via tool/iteration/token/time budgets.
  • Typed schemas and structured outputs.
  • Least-privilege tool permissions.
  • Deterministic money calculations.
  • Reproducible benchmark dataset and measurable outcomes.
  • Complete auditability without storing private chain-of-thought.

9. MVP Exit Criteria

P0 is complete when the happy path plus missing evidence, conflicting evidence, tool failure, duplicate recovery, unknown execution, policy block, budget exhaustion, and audit-integrity scenarios all behave as specified.
