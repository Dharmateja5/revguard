Concise ADRs capturing the rationale behind major design choices

Architecture Decision Records

Keep these short. The goal is to document why major choices were made and what was intentionally rejected.

ADR-001 — Separate probabilistic investigation from deterministic financial control

Decision: LLMs may reason about cases but may not perform authoritative financial math, policy overrides, or direct financial execution.

Why: Financial effects require reproducibility, exactness, and auditable control.

ADR-002 — Use Jev for fast structured routing with safe fallback

Decision: Jev handles classification/routing; malformed or unsupported output falls back to a conservative generative path.

Why: Reduce unnecessary generative reasoning while avoiding a single point of failure.

ADR-003 — Use MCP as the controlled integration boundary

Decision: Agents access enterprise data and actions through typed MCP tools rather than direct database or API calls.

Why: Centralize schemas, permissions, retries, idempotency, and audit metadata.

ADR-004 — Structured outputs at every agent/system boundary

Decision: Agent outputs must validate against typed Pydantic models before entering deterministic systems.

Why: Prevent prose from becoming an execution contract.

ADR-005 — Evidence provenance is separate from source authority

Decision: A verified claim can still be non-authoritative for a commercial decision.

Why: A statement may be genuine without having legal or policy authority.

ADR-006 — HITL is a risk boundary, not a UI feature

Decision: Missing/conflicting evidence, disputes, threshold breaches, unresolved commercial authority, and policy-required approvals route to HITL.

Why: Ambiguity should fail closed.

ADR-007 — Recovery uses explicit state, idempotency, and reconciliation

Decision: Unknown execution is reconciled before retry; duplicate actions are blocked through deterministic identity/idempotency.

Why: Financial side effects cannot rely on “retry and hope.”

ADR-008 — Voice is P1 evidence, not a new product surface

Decision: Pre-recorded calls are normalized to EvidenceItem and rejoin the existing pipeline.

Why: Demonstrates multimodal evidence without creating telephony or conversational-agent scope.

ADR-009 — No A2A / GraphRAG / production orchestration in MVP

Decision: Exclude technologies that do not solve a required P0 problem.

Why: Favor coherent architecture and a working end-to-end demo over technology accumulation.

ADR-010 — Implement durable-workflow semantics without adding a heavy workflow platform

Decision: Model durable state, retries, unknown execution, and reconciliation explicitly using the project’s state machines and persistence.

Why: Demonstrate the concepts while keeping hackathon scope manageable.
