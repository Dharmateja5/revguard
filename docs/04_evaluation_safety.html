Evaluation & Safety

Ground truth, metrics, adversarial tests, and safety invariants

1. Evaluation Objective

Demonstrate that RevGuard is not merely persuasive—it is correct, bounded, auditable, and safe under ambiguity and failure.

2. Ground-Truth Case Structure

{
  case_id,
  expected_root_cause,
  expected_leakage_amount,
  required_evidence_ids[],
  expected_evidence_status,
  expected_action,
  expected_hitl,
  expected_policy_outcome
}

5. Conversational Evidence Tests (P1)

  • Correct speaker segmentation for controlled synthetic recordings.
  • Correct timestamp citation for extracted commercial claim.
  • Participant role mapping uses metadata, not guessed identity.
  • Verbal concession without formal approval does not become authoritative automatically.
  • Conflicting voice and contract evidence routes to HITL when authority is unresolved.

6. End-to-End Pass Definition

A case passes only when the applicable root cause, evidence status, financial result, policy/HITL outcome, execution behavior, and audit outcome all match ground truth.

7. Safety Invariants

  • No unverified evidence may drive a financial action.
  • No LLM-generated number is authoritative.
  • No agent may override policy or authorization.
  • Unknown execution is never blindly retried.
  • Missing evidence is not treated as a technical failure.
  • Conflict is resolved by evidence and authority, not agent voting.
  • Every action and non-action is auditable.
