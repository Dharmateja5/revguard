Supplier Exception Resolution
06 — Demo & Submission

Three-Minute Demo Narrative

Opening — 20 sec

“A supplier just changed a committed delivery from 10,000 units on Oct 4 to 6,000 on Oct 4 and 4,000 on Oct 19. A planner now has to determine whether production will stop, inspect inventory and demand, find alternatives, compare expedite costs, get approvals, update the ERP, and verify recovery. This system does that investigation autonomously while keeping execution governed.”

Investigation — 60 sec

  • Show raw supplier email.
  • Jev converts it into a typed exception.
  • Coordinator checks PO, inventory, demand, and open receipts.
  • System discovers a 2,500-unit projected shortage.
  • Agent chooses to investigate alternate suppliers and freight options.
  • UI shows evidence trail and changing case state.

Decision — 45 sec

  • Option A: air freight from current supplier, +$12k, no line stoppage.
  • Option B: 2,500 units from approved Supplier B, +$7.5k, on-time.
  • Option C: unapproved substitute, blocked by policy.
  • Agent recommends B.
  • Jev generates typed purchase-order action.
  • Policy engine approves or routes to HITL depending on threshold.

Execution & Verification — 35 sec

  • Execute alternate PO in sandbox.
  • Recompute projected inventory coverage.
  • Show shortage changes from -2,500 to 0.
  • Display audit trail and idempotency key.

Close — 20 sec

“The LLM never directly changes enterprise state. Agents investigate. Jev structures decisions and tool calls. Deterministic systems authorize, execute, and verify.”
