Benchmark Design

Recommended 15 cases:

  • 3 straightforward partial-delivery cases.
  • 3 cases requiring alternate supplier search.
  • 2 cases where expedite is optimal.
  • 2 cases where no intervention is needed.
  • 2 HITL cases due to spend threshold or ambiguity.
  • 1 policy-blocked unqualified supplier.
  • 1 duplicate-trigger/idempotency case.
  • 1 unknown-execution/reconciliation case.

Ground Truth

Each case should specify known facts, valid recovery-action set, forbidden actions, expected shortage before/after, required evidence, expected HITL outcome, and allowed termination states.
