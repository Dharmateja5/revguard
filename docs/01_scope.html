Supplier Exception Resolution
01 — Scope

Project Scope

A bounded agentic workflow for supplier commitment failures. P0 focuses on quantity/date shortfalls for a single material and PO, with recovery through partial acceptance, expedite, alternate sourcing, or HITL.

Problem Boundary

Trigger: a supplier communicates that it cannot meet a previously committed quantity and/or delivery date.
Goal: determine operational impact, gather missing evidence, generate feasible recovery options, select or escalate the best plan, execute approved sandbox actions, and verify that projected shortage/service risk is resolved.

P0

  • Supplier email or structured event indicating quantity/date exception.
  • One purchase order, one material, one plant.
  • Inventory, near-term demand, shipment status, supplier availability, alternate suppliers, freight options.
  • Recovery actions: accept partial, expedite, split source, create alternate PO, escalate.
  • Jev for routing and typed tool calls.
  • Nemotron for adaptive investigation and recovery planning.
  • FastMCP tools over synthetic enterprise systems.
  • Deterministic shortage math, policy checks, authorization, and execution validation.
  • HITL for high-cost, unqualified, policy-blocked, or ambiguous actions.
  • Sandbox writes only, idempotent execution, post-action verification, audit trail.

P1

  • Quality exception / supplier nonconformance.
  • Approved substitute materials.
  • Multiple plants and customer-service priorities.
  • Repeated supplier updates causing replanning.
  • Parallel investigation across inventory, sourcing, and logistics.

Explicit Exclusions

  • No live ERP writes.
  • No autonomous supplier negotiation or external email sending in P0.
  • No global supply-chain optimization.
  • No probabilistic demand forecasting.
  • No full transportation management system.
  • No real procurement network integration.

Success Definition

P0 succeeds when the system can correctly investigate a supplier commitment exception, choose only policy-valid recovery actions, safely execute approved sandbox changes, and verify that the resulting operational state resolves or accurately escalates the shortage.
